# Proposed Community Edition and commercial roadmap

The current release uses the [Proprietary Commercial and Evaluation License](../LICENSE): personal noncommercial learning, research, and evaluation are permitted within its terms; commercial use and redistribution require separate written permission. The proposals below do not grant additional rights or change that license. See the [commercial terms](../COMMERCIAL_LICENSING.md) and [disclaimer](../DISCLAIMER.md).

| Offering | Proposed scope | Licensing direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Community Edition | Taxonomy, explorer, community tools, API interfaces, local REI and 1.2.2 core features | Consider AGPLv3 or another suitable license after rights/dependency review |
| Commercial services | Managed AI agents, advanced tutoring, institutional administration, private deployments | Reviewed SaaS terms or commercial software license |
| Enterprise services | Monthly subscriptions, institutional contracts, professional services | Agreements appropriate to deployment/support |

Release 1.2.2 adds no paywall or subscription billing. Labels/directories alone do not establish license compatibility. API interfaces do not grant access to private data or third-party content rights.

Before commercialization:

1. Confirm ownership/contributor rights and audit third-party code, taxonomy, data, and content.
2. Review any proposed future Community license and commercial boundaries before changing the current license.
3. Prepare applicable EULA, SaaS terms, privacy policy, and enterprise agreements.
4. Establish the business entity and verify registration, tax, and permit obligations.
5. Search trademarks for OpenBEXI Discovery before substantial branding investment.
6. Start with adult learners/researchers; design safeguards before school/children's accounts. Foundation is a knowledge level, not an age category.

Have the structure and final documents reviewed by a software/IP licensing professional before publication. The proposed launch combines a Community Edition and paid hosting, followed by institution/enterprise licensing when demand supports it. These planning tasks are separate from the local release.
