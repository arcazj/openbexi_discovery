# Video transcript: Create your first profile

## 00:00 — Make the workspace yours

Make OpenBEXI Discovery your own. Start in the installed local app, then create a profile for your research.

## 00:07 — Connect your AI

Choose Connect AI. Select your provider, paste your API key, and save. OpenAI is the default.

## 00:16 — Create new profile

The key step is Create new profile. Open Settings, then choose the button at the top.

## 00:22 — Describe your field

Describe your field or area of interest, not just a title. For example: understand dark matter, dark energy, and the expanding universe.

## 00:31 — Choose reasoning, then create

Choose a reasoning level from Medium to Ultra. Higher levels may take longer and use more tokens. Then create your profile.

## 00:40 — Your profile is ready

AI chooses a name and builds your overview, goals, and topics. This demonstration uses an example response.

## 00:48 — Review your profile

Review the orange AI-generated overview and the topic checkboxes. Your new profile opens its own workspace.

## 00:55 — Ask your first question

Write a question. Overall Search is broad. Specific Search focuses on checked topics or sources. For web search, use OpenAI.

## 01:05 — Return to your workspace

Switch profiles in Settings. Your selected profile returns when you reopen the local app.

## 01:12 — Create your profile. Then explore.

Always open the sources and verify the results. Start with a clear field. Create your profile. Then explore.

The AI response in this recording is illustrative. Actual generation time and output depend on your provider, model, reasoning level, and request.
